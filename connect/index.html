The model

An intelligent financial model for community mobility.

LUUP turns underused vehicles into shared financial assets. Owners can earn more predictable monthly income from vehicles they already own, while Members get reliable access to a trusted car without taking on the full cost of ownership.

01

Unlock value from existing cars

Owners can generate recurring income from vehicles that would otherwise sit underused.

02

Lower the cost of access

Members pay only for the access they need, without taking on the full financial burden of owning a vehicle.

03

Make sharing more predictable

With LUUP, owners and members build trust over time. It’s more stable and reliable than a typical one-time rental.
